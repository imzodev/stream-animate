Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, the optional answers you pick in the form (what you use Saidcue for, and which Pro feature interests you), the date, and the campaign tag in the link you came from, if any. We do not run advertising trackers on this page.

Why

To email you when Saidcue opens, and to decide what to build first. We do not sell your email or share it for marketing.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare, which hosts this site and its database. Cloudflare also processes basic request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site, under its own privacy policy.

How long

Until the waitlist closes or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Email hello@saidcue.com to see or delete your data, or to unsubscribe.

The Saidcue app

This page covers the website and waitlist only. The app is not released yet, and we will publish its privacy details, including what the AI co-pilot sends to our servers, before launch.
