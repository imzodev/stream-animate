Voice commands

Start with computer, then say what you want. Normal talk never triggers anything.

  • computer,skip ad

    Clicks YouTube's Skip button, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French or German.

  • computer,open youtube

    Opens a site or a program such as Notepad or the calculator.

  • computer,click subscribe

    Presses the button or link with that label, or puts the cursor in a text box.

  • computer,scroll down

    Scrolls the front window. Page up, top and bottom work too.

  • computer,volume to 50

    Sets the exact level. Mute and unmute are exact, not toggles.

  • computer,press enter

    Submits the form or message you just typed. “Submit” and “send it” work too.

Questions

Before you install.

What can I control by voice?

Anything with a keyboard shortcut, plus any button, link or text box that Windows UI Automation can see, which includes web pages in your browser. You can open sites and programs, scroll, set the volume, use media keys and press Enter. The full list is in the Voice Commands tab of the settings window.

Does it work on Linux?

Voice commands are Windows-only for now. The Linux build is an early release focused on dictation, voice triggers and the AI co-pilot, and it has had less testing.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 64-bit PC, or a 64-bit Linux desktop, with 8 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended) and a microphone. An NVIDIA GPU makes dictation faster on Windows, and it runs on the CPU if you don't have one. See the full requirements above.

Will it fire when I'm just talking?

No. A command only runs when your phrase starts with the wake word, so ordinary conversation is ignored. A two-word wake word such as “hey computer” misfires even less.

What leaves my computer?

Your voice is transcribed on your PC. Dictation, triggers and built-in voice commands send nothing. When you ask the AI co-pilot, or a voice command goes to the AI fallback, the text goes to the AI provider you configured.

Do I need my own AI account?

Only for the AI parts, and today, yes. Add an API key for any OpenAI-compatible provider, or point Saidcue at a local model. A hosted option with no key to set up is planned for Pro.

Why is the AI slow to answer?

Reasoning models think before they write. Set Model reasoning to Off or Low in the LLM settings, or pick a model that doesn't reason.

Will my viewers see the AI panel?

The panel is a normal window on your screen. If you capture your whole display or share your screen, people will see it. If you capture a single game or window, they won't.

Does it work with OBS and Streamlabs?

Overlays are drawn on top of your screen, so any capture setup that sees your display or game sees them. Direct scene control for OBS is planned, not available yet.

Is it safe to install?

The builds are not code-signed yet, which is why Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm. The source is public on GitHub, and the installers are built from it.

What about Mac?

Windows and Linux are available now. Mac is on the list after launch.
