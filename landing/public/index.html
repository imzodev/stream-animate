Questions

Before you join

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and a microphone. An NVIDIA GPU makes dictation faster, and it runs on the CPU if you don't have one.

Do I have to press hotkeys?

Voice triggers and dictation can run hands-free. The AI co-pilot uses one hotkey so it only listens for a question when you mean to ask one.

What leaves my computer?

Your voice is transcribed on your PC. When you ask the AI co-pilot something, only the text of that question is sent to get an answer: to our servers. Dictation and voice triggers send nothing.

Will my viewers or audience see the AI panel?

The panel is a normal window on your screen. If you capture your whole display or share your screen, people will see it. If you capture a single game or window, they won't.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. The AI co-pilot is hosted for you, so there is no key or account to set up. Free includes 30 questions a month, and Pro removes the limit.

What is the cloud library?

Ready-made packs of trigger phrases, sounds and overlays you can add in one click, plus your own settings synced between PCs. It is planned for Pro and not built yet.

Does it work with OBS and Streamlabs?

Overlays are drawn on top of your screen, so any capture setup that sees your display or game sees them. Direct scene control for OBS is planned, not available yet.

What about Mac?

Windows comes first. Mac is on the list after launch.
